# DilMart Store — M3 Final Closure Report

## Status

**M3 Closed**

M3 goal: transform operations from single-merchant assumptions into governed multi-merchant commercial readiness.

---

## 1) Delivered Batches

- **M3.0** Merchant operations audit and gap mapping
- **M3.1** Store readiness contract + activation guard + UI coverage
- **M3.2** Product readiness + catalog quality visibility + activation safeguards
- **M3.3** Merchant cockpit clarity (actions, signals, priorities)
- **M3.4** Catalog management tightening (payload integrity + scoped uniqueness)
- **M3.5** Merchandising controls (flags/offer validity rules)
- **M3.6** Coupon/offer commercial clarity (validation + scoped uniqueness + clearer UX)
- **M3.7** Platform vs merchant boundary signaling
- **M3.8** Lightweight admin governance layer
- **M3.9** Commercial readiness rulebook closure

---

## 2) Core Outcomes

- Multi-merchant operations are now explicit and scope-aware.
- Readiness is measurable, visible, and enforced server-side.
- Activation gates now align with commercial integrity.
- Admin and merchant dashboards support daily operational triage.
- Governance is documented with practical routines and ownership map.

---

## 3) Key Governance Capabilities Now Present

- Merchant activation blocked when store readiness incomplete
- Product activation/publish blocked when product readiness incomplete
- Coupon setup guarded against invalid business states
- Platform-only controls clearly separated from merchant scope
- Admin dashboard contains lightweight governance KPIs

---

## 4) Residual Risks / Next Focus

- SLA automation (alerts/escalations) remains lightweight/manual
- Exception management still procedural, not yet workflow-automated
- Advanced governance analytics can be expanded in future phases

---

## 5) M3 Verdict

**M3 is successfully closed as a commercial-readiness foundation.**

The platform is now materially more reliable for multi-merchant operations, with enforceable rules, clearer boundaries, and actionable governance visibility.
