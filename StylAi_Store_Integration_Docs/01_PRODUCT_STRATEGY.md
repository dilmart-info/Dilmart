# DilMart Store Integration — Product Strategy

## 1. Primary Direction

The first integration must focus on the Barber App, not the Customer App.

```txt
B2B Barber Store Integration First
```

The Store should become a strategic extension of the Barber App: a place where barbers and salon owners can buy trusted professional tools, salon equipment, consumables, and setup packages.

## 2. Product Positioning

### Customer side, future

```txt
Book and care for yourself.
```

### Barber/salon side, first priority

```txt
Manage, grow, and equip your salon from trusted suppliers.
```

## 3. Why B2B First

Salon owners and barbers have higher commercial value because they need repeated and professional purchasing:

- machines
- scissors
- razors
- sterilization products
- towels and capes
- salon chairs
- mirrors
- beauty devices
- nail tools
- setup packages
- wholesale supply

This creates repeat business and strengthens DilMart's role as an operating platform, not only a booking app.

## 4. Main User Segments

### Verified salon owner

Comes from DilMart Barber App and has a linked barbershop/salon.

Receives the strongest B2B experience:

- full salon setup products
- wholesale offers
- quote request
- salon-address checkout
- business-specific campaigns

### Barber staff

Comes from Barber App but is not the owner.

Receives professional personal tools:

- machines
- scissors
- razors
- combs
- professional hair/beard products

Should not primarily see chairs, mirrors, or full salon setup packages unless allowed by rules.

### Public retail buyer

Comes to the web store directly.

Can buy retail products without needing a DilMart app account.

### Public professional barber buyer

A barber who enters the web store but is not verified in DilMart.

Can see professional products but not full verified B2B benefits.

### Salon owner lead

An external salon owner not yet using DilMart.

Should be allowed to browse/buy, but also be converted into DilMart Barber App onboarding.

## 5. Commercial Rule

```txt
Buying is open to everyone.
Full professional B2B benefits are reserved for verified app-linked barbers and salon owners.
```

## 6. Future Customer App Direction

Customer App store integration is a later phase.

Future customer store can include:

- personal hair care
- beard care
- skincare
- home-use tools
- post-booking recommendations
- AI product assistant
- AI personalization by interest, gender, booking history, and browsing behavior

Do not include AI personalization in the first B2B MVP.
